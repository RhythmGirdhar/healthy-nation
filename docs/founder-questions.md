# Questions for the founder

Please confirm these details so we can finish the website without guessing. Short answers are fine.

1. Is the 55-item PDF menu still current? Which prices or items have changed, and do listed prices include tax?
2. Which 3-6 dishes should we recommend to a first-time customer? Please include vegetarian and chicken options.
3. For each dish, can you provide a short description, portion size, main ingredients, allergens and supported changes? How should customers raise allergy concerns?
4. Please match the supplied food photos to dish names. The files have date-only names, so exact matches need your confirmation. May we publish all these photos? Can you also provide an approved founder portrait?
5. Which areas or PIN codes do you deliver to? Is pickup available, and what public address can we show?
6. What are the opening days, ordering hours, order cutoff and usual preparation or delivery time?
7. What are the delivery fees, minimum order and any free-delivery rules? Are there other charges?
8. Who monitors WhatsApp, what response time should we state, and what message confirms that an order is accepted?
9. Which payment methods do you accept through the WhatsApp process? What are the cancellation, refund and missing-item rules?
10. Are meal plans available now? Please provide names, prices, meal counts, duration, delivery schedule, choices, skip or pause rules and cancellation terms.
11. Is there a weekly menu? How often do dishes change, and who will tell us when an item is unavailable?
12. What short founder story, business name, food-business registration details and contact information are approved for public display?
13. Which should be the main website domain: **thehealthynation.in** or **healthynation.co.in**? Hosting and DNS will remain unchanged for now.

V1 has no website checkout or online payment. Customers review their selection, send it on WhatsApp, and wait for team confirmation.
